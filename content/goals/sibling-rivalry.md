Brothers and sisters squabbling is a normal part of growing up. How siblings treat each other starts with how the family treats each other, and a little focused attention for each child can take some heat out of the competition.

## Win: Give each child a little one-on-one time

### Action
Find a few minutes each day when one child has your full attention, without their siblings. Fit it into your routine, like a short chat at bedtime or a walk, and take turns so every child gets their moment.

### Say this
"This is our special time, just you and me. What do you want to tell me about today?"

### What to expect
Children often compete less for your attention when they know their own time is coming. It doesn't need to be long; doing it regularly is what counts. When fights do happen, stay a calm, neutral listener and give them a chance to sort it out themselves first, stepping in if they can't or if someone could get hurt.
