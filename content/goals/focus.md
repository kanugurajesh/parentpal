Young children practice paying attention through play. Simple listening games cost nothing, take a few minutes, and give your child a fun reason to listen carefully and wait before acting.

## Win: Play Simon Says

### Action
Play a few rounds of Simon Says: your child only follows an instruction when it starts with "Simon says". Start slowly with easy moves like touching your nose or jumping, then go a little faster as they get the hang of it. Let your child have a turn being Simon too.

### Say this
"Listen carefully: only move if you hear 'Simon says'. Ready? Simon says touch your nose!"

### What to expect
The American Academy of Pediatrics notes that Simon Says encourages good listening and focus. Young children will often move without hearing "Simon says" at first. Laugh it off and keep going. Short, playful rounds a few times a week work better than one long game.
