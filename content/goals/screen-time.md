Clear, predictable family rules make screen time easier for everyone than deciding moment by moment. The American Academy of Pediatrics suggests a simple family media plan: when screens are used, where they're not, and what fills the rest of the day.

## Win: Pick screen-free times and places

### Action
Choose one or two screen-free zones and times, such as the dinner table and the hour before bed, and keep them the same every day. Keep screens for regular times of day rather than whenever they're asked for. Grown-ups follow the same rules, since children copy what we do.

### Say this
"In our family, the table is for talking. Screens wait in the basket until after dinner."

### What to expect
Expect some pushback in the first days while the new rule becomes normal. Keeping it the same every day matters more than how strict it is. Fill the time with something your child enjoys, like reading together, a game or time outside.
